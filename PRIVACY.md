# Little Skies: privacy

Little Skies collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and
no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or your device anywhere.

Your progress (the holes you have played and your scores) is saved on your
device only, and is removed when you uninstall the game.

The web version at https://little-skies.helder.run is served by helder.run,
which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for operating the
service; the game itself sends nothing.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/helder/little-skies/issues.
